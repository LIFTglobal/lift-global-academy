Step-by-step guidance for using LIFT Global Academy
Click a question to expand it. What you see below is tailored to your account type — sections only appear if they're relevant to you.
Getting around
How do I switch between light and dark mode?
Click the "Dark mode" switch near the bottom of the sidebar. Your choice is remembered on this device, so you won't need to set it again next time you sign in.
How do I navigate between different parts of the program?
Use the links in the sidebar on the left — they take you straight to any page you have access to. On a phone or narrow screen, tap the menu icon in the top bar to open the sidebar.

You can also collapse the sidebar to just icons using the "Collapse" button at the bottom, if you want more room to work.
How do I sign out?
Click "Sign out" in the top-right corner of any page.
Getting started as a teacher
Which workspace should I use — Secondary, Middle, or Elementary?
From your Home page, pick whichever matches how you teach:
  • Secondary / high school — you teach one subject at a time to specific classes, one student at a time.
  • Middle school — similar, organized around the subjects you teach.
  • Elementary classroom — you teach one class all subjects together; you'll grade a student across all subjects at once instead of picking a class first.
You can switch anytime by returning to your Home page.
How do I submit a grade and evaluation?
  1. Go to "Submit grades" in the sidebar.
  2. Choose your class (Secondary/Middle), or choose a student directly (Elementary).
  3. Choose the student, enter their grade, a written evaluation, and any comments for LIFT.
  4. Optionally attach a document (report card, assessment, writing sample, etc.).
  5. Click "Save as draft" to keep working on it later, or "Submit for review" once you're ready to send it to LIFT.
You can track the status of everything you've submitted under "Your submissions" — Draft, Pending review, Approved, or Revisions requested.
How does the Gradebook work?
The Gradebook lets you track ongoing assignments instead of typing in one final grade from memory.
  1. Go to "Gradebook" and pick a class (or subject, for Elementary).
  2. You'll see default grading categories (Homework, Classwork, Tests/Quizzes, Final/Project) already set up with standard weights — rename them, change their weights, or add your own to fit how you grade.
  3. Add assignments under a category with a title and points possible.
  4. Enter each student's score as you grade their work. Use the "N/A" button on any assignment a student is excused from — it won't count against their average.
  5. Each student's running average updates automatically based on your category weights.
  6. When you're ready to submit a grade, click "Use in submission" next to a student — it jumps you to the Submit grades form with their average already filled in, so you can review and submit.
How do I take attendance?
Go to "Attendance," choose a class and date (Secondary/Middle) or just a date (Elementary, since it's one classroom all day), then click Present, Absent, Tardy, or Excused next to each student. A running count for the day appears above the list. You can come back and change a mark anytime.
What do the submission statuses mean?
  • Draft — saved, but not yet sent to LIFT. Only you can see it.
  • Pending review — submitted and waiting for a LIFT evaluator to look at it.
  • Approved — reviewed and accepted; credit has been awarded and it now counts toward the student's graduation progress and transcript.
  • Revisions requested — the evaluator needs something changed or clarified before it can be approved.
Getting started as a school administrator
How do I add teachers and other administrators?
Go to "Teachers" or "Administrators" in your school dashboard and use the "Add" form — you'll set their name, email, and a temporary password. For teachers, you can also assign an elementary grade level right away if that applies to them. New accounts can sign in immediately with the credentials you set.
How do I set up a class for a middle/high school teacher?
Go to "Classes," create a class with its subject, grade level, term, and the teacher who'll run it, then use "Manage roster" to check off which students belong in it. Elementary teachers don't need a class — they're scoped to a whole grade level instead, set on their account under "Teachers."
How do I add or edit students?
Go to "Students" to add a new student or edit an existing one — name, grade level, date of birth, gender, and parent/guardian contact info. Use the school filter at the top if you manage more than one school's worth of students.
How do I record a student's history at a previous school?
Go to "School history," pick the student, and add an entry with the prior school's name, dates attended, attendance record, and address. This information is used when building that student's official transcript.
How do I pay an invoice from LIFT?
Go to "Billing" — any unpaid invoices from LIFT show a "Pay via PayPal" button. Once you pay, LIFT will mark it as received on their end.
How do I complete onboarding?
Go to "Onboarding" — you'll see any documents LIFT needs you to sign and any tasks they've asked you to complete.
  • For each document: open and read it, check the box confirming you've read it, type your full legal name, then click Sign (or Acknowledge, for documents that don't require a formal signature).
  • For each task: click "Mark complete" once it's done. You can undo this if you marked something by mistake.
Getting started as LIFT staff
How do I review a teacher's submission?
Go to "Review queue" and open any pending submission. Read the grade, evaluation, and any attached documents, then either:
  • Approve — choose the graduation category and the credit value to award, then click Approve. This is what makes it count toward the student's graduation progress and transcript.
  • Request revisions — leave a note explaining what's missing or needs correcting; it goes back to the teacher as a draft they can resubmit.
How do I manage partner schools, their teachers, and their students?
All of this lives under "Manage schools," which has its own set of tabs: Schools (add/edit schools, logos, branding, accreditation and membership status), Teachers, Administrators, Classes, Students, and School history. School administrators can manage most of the same things for their own school only — you can see and manage every school.
How do I enter a student's grades from a school they attended before joining LIFT?
Under "Manage schools" → "School history," pick the student and open the "Transfer credits" panel below their school history entries. Add each prior class with its grade, the graduation category it counts toward, and the credit value — it's marked approved immediately and counts toward that student's graduation progress right away, with no teacher or school involvement needed.
How do I onboard a new partner school?
Under "Manage schools" → "Onboarding":
  1. Build your reusable template library once — document templates (upload a file, and mark whether it requires a signature) and task templates.
  2. Pick the new school, check off which templates apply, and click "Assign selected."
  3. Track their progress on the same page — how many documents are signed and tasks completed — until they're fully onboarded.
The school administrator completes their side from their own "Onboarding" page.
How do I create and manage invoices?
Go to "Billing," choose a school, and create an invoice with a description and amount. Once the school pays, mark it paid — or void it if it shouldn't have been issued.
How does graduation progress and transcript generation work?
Both pull from every approved submission for a student — drafts and pending items never count. "Graduation progress" totals credits earned by category against LIFT's requirement set. "Transcript" lists every approved course with its grade and credit, calculates a GPA, and lets you generate an official PDF.
How do I preview what a teacher sees?
From your Home page, open "Teacher workspace (preview)," then pick Secondary, Middle, or Elementary. At the top of that page, use the "Previewing as" dropdown to pick any teacher account. Everything you do while previewing — assignments, scores, attendance, submissions — is saved under that teacher's account, exactly as if they'd done it. Use "Exit preview" in the top-right to return to your own evaluator home.
